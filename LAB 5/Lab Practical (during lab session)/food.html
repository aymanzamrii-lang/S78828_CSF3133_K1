About the Festival

Taste of Terengganu brings together the best local food stalls for three days of eating,music and culture by the river. Visitor can try famous dishes such as nasi dagang, kerepok lekor and laksam , prepared by home cooks and small businesses from all over the state.

Free entry for all UMT students - show your matric card at the entrance
