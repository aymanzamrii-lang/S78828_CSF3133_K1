Welcome to UMT

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT) is a public university located in Kuala Nerus, Terengganu, Malaysia. It is known for its focus on marine science, aquatic resources and maritime studies.

About UMT

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu is a university that provides higher education and research opportunities in various fields of science, technology and management.

UMT focuses on developing knowledgeable and skilled graduates through academic excellence, research activities and innovation.

The university also contributes to the development of marine science, oceanography, fisheries and environmental sustainability.
